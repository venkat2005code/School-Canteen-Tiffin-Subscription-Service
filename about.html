Our Story

About NourishKid

We believe that healthy food is the foundation of a great education. Founded by pediatric dietitians and chefs, NourishKid aims to revolutionize school lunches.

Our Principles

Our Core Values

The guiding principles that dictate how we operate, who we partner with, and how we serve our community every single day.

Child First

Every decision we make prioritizes the health and development of the students we serve. We never compromise on nutrition.

Total Transparency

From ingredient sourcing to kitchen hygiene, parents have full visibility into what their kids eat through our app.

Sustainability

Eco-friendly packaging, local farm partnerships, and optimized route planning to minimize our carbon footprint.

Our Mission

To provide nutritious, delicious, and safe meals to every student, while making canteen management effortless for schools.

By partnering directly with local farms and employing top-tier pediatric dietitians, we are setting a new standard for what children eat during their most crucial developmental hours. We believe that good food is the ultimate foundation for a great education.

Our Journey

How we grew from a small community kitchen to a city-wide network.

2020

Founded by pediatric dietitians noticing a critical gap in healthy, accessible school lunches.

2022

Launched the RFID Cashless Canteen pilot program, expanding our footprint across 5 major schools.

2025

Expanded to a state-of-the-art central kitchen to serve over 14,000 daily meals with automated logistics.
