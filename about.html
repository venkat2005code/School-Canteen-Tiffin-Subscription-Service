Our Story

About NourishKid

We believe that healthy food is the foundation of a great education. Founded by pediatric dietitians and chefs, NourishKid aims to revolutionize school lunches.

Our Mission

To provide nutritious, delicious, and safe meals to every student, while making canteen management effortless for schools.

Our Core Values

Child First

Every decision we make prioritizes the health and development of the students we serve.

Total Transparency

From ingredient sourcing to kitchen hygiene, parents have full visibility into what their kids eat.

Sustainability

Eco-friendly packaging and optimized route planning to minimize our carbon footprint.

Our Journey

How we grew from a small community kitchen to a city-wide network.
